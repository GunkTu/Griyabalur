# DESIGN.md: Griya Balur Bukian Mertasari

Direction source: PRD v1.1 section 06 ("Natural Science, Refined & Human"). Applied through antislop (mode: during).

**Design Read:** profil dan edukasi untuk calon pengunjung, keluarga pendamping, dan komunitas, dalam bahasa visual editorial ilmu-alam (hijau hutan + porselen, foto asli, tipografi tegas), dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 3 / MOTION 2.

## Palette (R-29: 2 core + 1 accent + neutrals)
- Core 1: Deep Forest `#193B32` (headings, panel, nav). Reason: PRD token, reads as calm and botanical.
- Core 2: Sage `#84946D` (marks, quiet surfaces). Reason: PRD token, bridges forest and porcelain.
- Accent: Warm Copper `#98582A` for buttons (darkened from PRD `#B46D38` so white text passes 4.5:1). Used only on the main action and small link hovers.
- Neutrals: Porcelain `#F7F5EF`, Charcoal `#20302D`, tint `#ECE9DD`.

## Typography (R-06)
Plus Jakarta Sans only (PRD names it). Weight and size carry hierarchy: 800 for the hero, 700 for headings, 400 for text. One typographic voice: a single word in each major heading is set in italic of the same family.
No uppercase wide-tracked labels.

## Identity motif
Terrace contour lines: thin wavy lines stacked like rice terraces. They echo the Bali landscape in the photos and the PRD's "thin biophysics line pattern". Used in three places only: the visit panel, the contact section, the footer. Never as a generic background grid (R-07).

## Shape system (R-11)
Controls 8px, images and surfaces 14px, large panels 24px. The floating nav is the only pill.

## Dials
- ENERGY 2: calm, photo-led, one big hero moment.
- RHYTHM 3: every section has its own composition (statement + wide photo, typographic index, sticky photo + accordion, definition list in a panel, tab viewer, masonry, plain text, FAQ split, contact band).
- MOTION 2: fade-up once on scroll, hover color shifts, tab image crossfade. No loops.

## Decisions with reasons (R-31)
- Hero is a full-bleed photo: the place is the product, and the PRD asks for a real location photo.
- BALUR concepts as a typographic index, not cards: three abstract ideas have no visual, so type does the work.
- Facilities as a tab viewer: five rooms, one large photo at a time, so each room is seen properly (replaces a bento mosaic).
- Gallery as masonry with natural aspect ratios: photos keep their own proportions.
- Visit flow as a definition list: four real steps, no icons or numbers needed.
- No stats, testimonials, logos, or counters: none are real (R-17, R-18, R-38).
- Contact data, WhatsApp, and Maps are empty on purpose and shown as "coming soon" until the owner fills them (R-23, R-26).

## Deviations from the PRD copy (R-37, owner to confirm)
- CTA "Jelajahi Fasilitas / Explore Our Facilities" became "Lihat fasilitas / See the facilities", and "Hubungi Kami / Contact Us" became "Tanya admin / Ask our admin" (R-15: no generic "Explore" CTA).
- The glass cards and bento gallery in PRD section 06 were dropped (R-05, R-10).
